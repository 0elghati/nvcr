# DCVC-RT under `nvcr.fx.v1` (RTX 4070, 2026-11-12)

Branch `feat/dcvcrt-fx`, stacked on `feat/codec-numerics-profiles` (#248). One RTX 4070 (SM 8.9), Ubuntu 24.04,
CUDA 12.8, TensorRT 10.9, ONNX Runtime 1.26.0 (CPU), Triton 3.5.1 for the ahead-of-time kernels.

## What runs

- **Profile:** `nvcr.fx.v1` ([spec](../docs/spec/numerics-fx-v1.md)): all-integer fixed point, int32 values at 2^-14,
  two-digit int8 GEMMs with per-layer input shifts, round-half-up requantization, table activations.
- **Programs:** eleven DCVC-RT stages exported by `scripts/backends/dcvcrt/export_fx_programs.py` from the pinned
  cvpr2025 checkpoints, with `configs/numerics/dcvcrt-fx.json`. They carry DCVC-RT's spatial coding steps too: the
  masks, the scale-to-index rule (the index edges as a `sat_table`) and the y_hat update. The coder exchanges only
  entropy indexes, means and symbols with the provider.
- **FP16 stages:** the encoder's analysis transforms (`p_encode` reads the 8-bit picture and the exact context) and the
  P-frame display picture (`p_display`, from the exact feature). Nothing reads either back, as in the research codec.
  The exact picture of a P frame is computed only when a reference reset needs it.
- **Providers:**
  - TensorRT: plans compiled from the programs (`nvcr-fx-plans`, plugins `NvcrFxGemm`, `NvcrFxDepthwise`,
    `NvcrFxOp`);
  - ONNX Runtime CPU: NVCR's reference executor.
- **Bundles:** the six DCVC-RT engine profiles (qcif, cif, 360p, 540p, 720p, 1080p; TensorRT 10.9, RTX 4070), built by
  `scripts/backends/dcvcrt/build_tensorrt.sh` with `p_encode.plan` and `p_display.plan` (strongly typed). Speed and
  BD-rate below use the 1080p bundle. `scripts/backends/dcvcrt/add_fx_plans.py` adds fx plans over each bundle's
  visible range, like its native plans: dynamic from 64x64 to the maximum for qcif, cif, 720p and 1080p, fixed for
  360p and 540p.

## A fix to the research setting: the recon network's input shifts

The research policy `rt_hybrid_k1.json` has no entries for `recon_generation_net`, which the research ran only at
reference resets. Unlisted layers saturate their GEMM inputs at about ±2.0, while the recon network's inputs reach
5.9 to 14.1. With the exact recon drawing P pictures, P frames lost about 2 dB (frame 1 at QP 32: 35.77 dB against
38.10 native and 38.18 for the research codec).

The recon layers' ranges were measured with the research method (config B, Kimono + Johnny, 9 frames x 4 rates,
`dcvc-robust/scripts/probe_recon_range.py`), and the same one-bit headroom rule gives shifts of 1 to 4. They are in
`configs/numerics/dcvcrt-fx.json`. The exact recon now runs only at reference resets, as in the research codec, and
the same gap affects the research codec's reset path every 64 frames.

## Speed (diagnostic)

BasketballDrive 1080p, QP 21, 31 steady frames after the first, codec interval from the CLI, two runs agreeing within
1 %:

| Profile | Encode | Decode |
|---|---|---|
| native | 45.5 fps | 47.6 fps |
| `nvcr.fx.v1`, first version | 4.8 fps | 8.5 fps |
| `nvcr.fx.v1`, this branch | 24.5 fps (40.8 ms) | 29.4 fps (34.0 ms) |
| research codec (Python + Triton, hybrid + coarse) | 27.9 fps (35.9 ms) | 31.6 fps (31.7 ms) |

What changed from the first version:
- provider buffers reused across frames (pinned allocations cost about 35 ms per frame);
- TensorRT stages pinned host tensors through device copies instead of reading them over the bus;
- the spatial steps run in the programs, so params and y_hat never leave the GPU;
- P pictures from `p_display` (8-bit planes) and the encoder input through `p_encode`, as in the research codec.

A decoded P frame now spends 30.6 ms on the GPU (exact GEMMs 19.8 ms) and 4.6 ms on the host (mostly rANS). Per-shape
GEMM tiles were measured with the research kernel and gain 0.1 ms, so they are not used.

## Not checked

- A second GPU (RTX 5060), and any QNN or AMD implementation.
- A cross-provider decode longer than eight frames or across a reference reset.
- Frame sizes above 1920x1080 (no engine profile, as for native).
- An ONNX Runtime CPU encode decoded on TensorRT.
- Upstream-anchor BD-rate under NVCR (the comparison above is against NVCR native).
